    Worksheet 1.5. Green’s Theorem
    

    MTH 401

    Theorem 8.

    Green’s Theorem: For a two-dimensional field \(\mbf F = (P,Q)\) and a closed curve \(C\) that is oriented counterclockwise (positively oriented) and that encloses the region \(D\text{,}\) the work done by \(\mbf F\) along \(C\) is
    \begin{equation*} \ds \oint_C \mbf F \cdot ~d\mbf r = \iint_D \left(\frac{\partial Q}{\partial x} - \frac{\partial P}{\partial y}\right)~dA = \iint_D |\nabla \times \mbf F|~dA\text{.} \end{equation*}
    🔗
    🔗

    1.

    Use Green’s Theorem to evaluate the line integral \(\ds \oint_C -y~dx + x~dy\) where \(C\) is the unit circle oriented in the counterclockwise direction.
    🔗
